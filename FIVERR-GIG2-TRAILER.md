## Schritt 3 · Description & FAQ

**Description (1200 Zeichen max — das hier hat ~1010):**

```
Your campaign deserves a cold open.

I create cinematic dark fantasy trailers for D&D and TTRPG campaigns - the moody, painterly kind you would expect from a AAA game reveal. Tell me about your world: the villain, the party, the place where it all goes wrong. I turn it into a music-ready trailer your players will rewatch before every session.

What you get:
- Cinematic scenes built from your campaign descriptions (AI-generated, hand-curated - every frame reviewed and refined)
- Title cards with your campaign name, party or villain
- 1080p60 MP4, ready for Discord, YouTube, session-zero slides or your stream

Perfect for:
- Campaign kickoffs and session zero
- DM gifts - the most personal gift a game master can get
- Actual play shows and TTRPG streams

I run EndoCraft, a dark fantasy studio for tabletop content - same style as my stream overlay gig.

Transparency: visuals are AI-generated and individually curated, composed and edited by hand. If it is not dark enough, I will make it darker.
```

**FAQ (3× „Add FAQ", optional aber empfohlen):**

1. *Do I need to provide art or footage?* — No. Everything is created from your
   text descriptions. Links to character art or a campaign doc help me match
   your vision, but plain words are enough.
2. *Is the trailer AI-generated?* — The visuals are AI-generated and then
   hand-curated: I compose, select, edit and time every shot myself. You see
   the result before final delivery.
3. *Can you sync it to music or add my voice lines?* — Delivered music-ready
   (timed for a track you drop in). Licensed music is not included, but I can
   cut to a track you own or add your recorded voice lines.
