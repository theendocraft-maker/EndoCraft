## Description

THE FIRST NIGHT — A Dark Fantasy Campaign Starter Kit

Forty-six pages that take a table from "we should play something" to a first
session that actually starts on time. No stat blocks to convert, no setting to
learn: this is the prep, the tables and the paper you fill in.

WHAT IS INSIDE

Seven chapters, in the order you actually need them.

The Compact — ten questions for the table, ten for each player, and a one-page
agreement everyone signs before the first roll.

The World — the tone dial, faction sheets with a doom clock, a region map to
fill in yourself, and a page for the rot at the centre of it.

The Roads — twenty rumours with ten of them true and the key in the margin,
twelve omens, and one hundred encounters across four tables: on the road, in
the wilds, below, and in the house.

The People — an NPC in three rolls (who, what they want, what they hide),
twelve tells, forty names, and a ledger for everyone the party has met.

The Rot — a six-step corruption track, twelve boons, twelve curses, and the
rules for gaining and shedding it.

Between — carousing on a d20, twelve downtime projects, and twelve
complications for when the party stays too long in one place.

The Keeper — a prep sheet you fill in before the night, a page for what the
players actually did, and the questions you ask yourself afterwards.

BY THE NUMBERS

46 pages. 19 rollable tables. 363 individual entries, every one written for
this book. 12 full-page plates.

FORMATS

Both US Letter and A4 are included, laid out page for page so a group split
across two continents can print from either file and stay on the same page
number. The contents page is hyperlinked and every chapter is bookmarked in
the PDF outline, so on a tablet you tap once instead of scrolling past forty
pages mid-session. Works in GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf and Xodo.

SYSTEM NEUTRAL

There is nothing here to convert. No armour class, no saving throws, no
levels. Every table gives you a situation and leaves the mechanics to whatever
you already run — 5E, OSR, Shadowdark, Mork Borg, Dragonbane, or your own.

ABOUT THE ARTWORK

The twelve plates were generated with AI image tools, then selected, colour
graded, retouched and typeset by hand. The written content — all 363 table
entries, every prompt and every page of instruction — was written by a human
for this book. I would rather tell you that plainly than have you find out.
