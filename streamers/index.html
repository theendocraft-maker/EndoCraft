Two things you should know

Said Plainly

These are full-screen backgrounds

They are not transparent webcam frames, chat boxes or alert graphics. Each file fills the whole canvas; your camera, chat and labels go on top in OBS.

The imagery is AI-generated, then curated by hand

The raw frames come out of generative models — the curation is the craft: hundreds of candidates thrown away, the survivors colour-graded, retimed and cut into loops that match at the seam. If AI-assisted artwork is a line you do not cross, that is fair, and this is not for you.
